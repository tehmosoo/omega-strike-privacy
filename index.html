Privacy Policy for OMEGA STRIKE

Effective date: May 22, 2026

OMEGA STRIKE is a fitness app for tracking workouts, protocols, training history, and optional Apple Health integration.

Information We Collect

OMEGA STRIKE may store workout and app-related data that you enter, such as workout sessions, exercises, sets, reps, weights, protocol information, and related fitness history.

Health Data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, OMEGA STRIKE may read and/or write Health data only with your permission. This may include workout data and, in future versions or enabled features, other fitness-related metrics such as body measurements, steps, or distance. Health data is used only to provide app features and is not used for advertising, marketing, or sold to third parties.

How Your Data Is Used

  • To let you track workouts and training progress.
  • To display your training history and metrics.
  • To sync approved fitness information with Apple Health when enabled.
  • To improve app functionality and reliability.

Data Sharing

OMEGA STRIKE does not sell your personal data. OMEGA STRIKE does not share Health data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Data may be stored locally on your device and, where applicable, through Apple-provided services or systems you authorize. If future versions introduce cloud sync or additional data processing, this policy may be updated.

Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to connect Apple Health.
  • You can revoke Health permissions at any time in the Apple Health app.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.

Children’s Privacy

OMEGA STRIKE is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
