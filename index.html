OMEGA STRIKE Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OMEGA STRIKE handles information when you use the app. OMEGA STRIKE is a fitness and training app focused on workouts, protocols, and performance tracking.

1. Summary

OMEGA STRIKE is designed to keep most of your data on your device or in your own private Apple services. The app does not use advertising SDKs, does not use tracking across apps or websites, and does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

The main third-party service used by the app is RevenueCat, which helps manage subscriptions and in-app purchases.

2. Information the app uses

Workout and training data

The app may store workout logs, exercise selections, sets, body metrics, protocols, notes, and related training data. This information is used to provide the app’s core functionality.

Health and fitness data

If you choose to grant permission, the app may read and write certain HealthKit data, such as workouts and fitness-related metrics. This data is used only to provide health and fitness features inside the app.

Location data

If you choose to grant permission and use location-related workout features, the app may access location data for activities such as outdoor run tracking. This is optional and only used to support those features.

Subscription and purchase data

If you purchase a subscription or use in-app purchases, purchase-related information is processed through Apple and RevenueCat so the app can unlock and manage premium features.

3. How information is stored

Most app data is stored locally on your device. Some app data may also be stored in your personal private iCloud / CloudKit storage, which is controlled by your Apple account. We do not operate our own custom backend for storing your workout history or training logs.

4. Third-party services

RevenueCat

OMEGA STRIKE uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process purchase history and related subscription status information so the app can determine which premium features are available.

More information is available in RevenueCat’s privacy materials: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple services

The app may use Apple services such as HealthKit, CloudKit, StoreKit, CoreLocation, and related system frameworks to provide app features.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data broker purposes.

6. Permissions

Some features may request access to Apple frameworks such as HealthKit or location services. You can decline these permissions, and many parts of the app will still work. You can also change permissions later in the Settings app on your device.

7. Data retention

Data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or remove the app. Data stored in your private Apple cloud services remains subject to your Apple account settings and controls. Purchase and subscription information may also be retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own policies.

8. Children’s privacy

OMEGA STRIKE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
